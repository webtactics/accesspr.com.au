BASSINTHEGRASS is one of the biggest – and longest running – contemporary music festivals in the country.

It’s an annual festival ritual that draws fans from across the country to spend 12 hours at an unmatched beachside location with tropical palms, bankable blue skies and serene sunsets.
It not only delivers an unforgettable experience with serious national and international talent ever year, it’s also a major economic shot in the arm and a key major event in the NT visitor economy.

Access delivers national publicity in the run up to, and during, BASS, working closely with talent management, artists, producers and coordinating effective stakeholder management.
