 Parrtjima is the only Aboriginal event of its kind – a spectacular award-winning and free 10-night festival set in the breathtaking Red Centre.

Through Aboriginal art, cultures and storytelling, Parrtjima illuminates the world’s oldest living culture through immersive large-scale light installations and soundscapes, workshops, music, dance, and more since 2016.

Access PR has managed the publicity and reputation since inception, working closely with event producers, artists and government stakeholders.

This festival, along with the work we do with Tourism and Events NT and Darwin Festival, has given us a deep understanding of how to work respectfully and authentically with Aboriginal communities and cultures.
